# PumpShield release work — 10 November2026

## Implemented

- Separate Petrol(MS), Diesel(HSD), High Octane(HOBC) types, selectors, labels, passes, offline context and stock/price constraints. No fixed market rate or RON rating is inferred.
- Matched-grade nozzle choices for credit fills. Overdue/no-shift credit fills stop before asking staff for photos and link to shift management. Late shift closure remains available.
- Astryx0.7.11 and its neutral theme installed through the documented React19/Next integration. Initial controls use actual Astryx components; existing transaction forms and accessible Radix dialogs are preserved. This is progressive adoption, not conversion of every component.
- The installed shortcut opens the normal app. When its navigation fails offline, the worker serves the public device workspace without caching signed-in pages/APIs.
- Opt-in24-hour trusted-device access stores a nonextractable device credential, never the account password. Reload/installed offline launch resumes that credential; expiry/Lock/logout revoke it. Encryption and existing recovery phrases/drafts remain intact. This convenience reduces at-rest protection on an unlocked or compromised device and is intended for managed/personal phones.
- Automatic replay occurs when the app is open/unlocked and connectivity returns. A closed browser/PWA cannot promise background synchronization. Server credit, original shift, current authorization, evidence and replay checks still apply.
- Explicit workspace-creation intent also prevents the old deployed auto-join GET from creating membership during the database-before-UI cutover.
