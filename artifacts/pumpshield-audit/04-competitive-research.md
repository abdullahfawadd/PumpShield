# Competitive and visual research

Research date: 9 November 2026. This is a design transfer study, not a ranking of companies or a claim that their private applications were tested. Sources are primary product documentation, public illustrations, official accelerator directories and the live reference pages. [Browser research records](evidence/research-browser.json) retain URLs, titles, image metadata and observed access limitations. [Research captures](research/) are separate from PumpShield evidence.

## Method and limits

Eleven relevant products were researched below. Linear, Retool, PagerDuty and Segment documentation exposed identifiable product-interface screenshots that were captured and visually examined. Vercel documentation and Stripe Dashboard documentation were rendered; their first eligible image was also captured, but Stripe's automatic capture selected a resources banner rather than a payment-table screenshot. It must not be described as a payment-screen audit. GoSpotCheck exposed public workflow illustrations and product descriptions; this is product-page evidence, not an authenticated field session. Brex was inspected through detailed operational documentation. Browser access to Shippo and Flexport help pages reached bot challenges, while their public article text was available through web retrieval. T3's release-note page rendered but its embedded map image was not available to this browser capture. These gaps are retained, not silently counted as visual tests.

No exact competitor font family, HEX palette, mobile breakpoint or private-app behavior is inferred from a marketing page. The “color / type” column describes observed public material; implementation tokens for PumpShield are independently specified and measured in report05. Responsive patterns documented for a product are distinguished from those actually exercised on PumpShield.

Started with the official [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) and [principles](https://www.ycombinator.com/principles). Official company jobs pages explicitly show the six YC batches above. The principles emphasize useful products and feedback; they are not a dashboard style specification. Flock Safety and Weave were considered, but surveillance and healthcare communications add less value to this station's core workflows than the selected operations products; no visual findings are inferred from their association alone.

The [Techstars portfolio](https://www.techstars.com/portfolio) and [Logixboard listing](https://jobs.techstars.com/companies/logixboard) establish another relevant logistics candidate. Logixboard's cohort was not established from those sources, and no private interface was examined. The [500 portfolio](https://500.co/portfolio) is image-heavy; Shippo's backing is instead confirmed by the linked first-party500 article. “Batch8” was not retained because primary cohort confirmation was not found. [Antler's paginated portfolio](https://www.antler.co/portfolio?0b933bfd_page=4&8cea4155_page=2) lists Cloud Cycle; batch and actual quality-monitoring UI were not verified, so it is an additional industrial candidate rather than a scored interface reference.

## Broader visual references

- [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/): use hierarchy, contrast, predictable controls and generous targets. Liquid Glass is an optional material, not permission to reduce contrast or decorate every surface. Do not copy Apple trademarks or the Music glyph.
- [OpenAI public help/interface descriptions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10056348-finding-your-chats-projects-and-files-in-chatgpt): concise navigation, search and focused content. This audit does not assert private app measurements or accelerator history.
- [Flowing launch](https://flowing.to/launch): observed split composition, strong typography and dense motion artwork are suitable inspiration for a desktop login. They are not suitable for receipts, meter readings or a phone keypad. Current PumpShield already contains local Flowing media with attribution; verify reuse rights before additional extraction.
- [RonDesignLab](https://dribbble.com/RonDesignLab), [Behance operations work](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/operations%20dashboard), [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/): references for grouping, material restraint and composition. Portfolio shots are proposals, not proof of production usability, responsive states or accessibility. No claim of award-level certification is made.
- [Phosphor React](https://github.com/phosphor-icons/react) is already installed. Preserve consistent family/weight and meaningful labels instead of adopting a second “trending” set. [Glisse](https://github.com/0kzh/glisse) is already installed at0.5.0; its visual material should remain limited and receive reduced-motion/contrast/keyboard checks. Demo URL retrieval failed in the web tool; the repository and installed package remain available. No dependency was installed or upgraded during this audit.

## Transferable conclusions

1. **Work queue before illustration.** PagerDuty's triage and Brex's review model transfer directly to arrival/shift approvals. A large tanker does not help decide whether 124L needs review.
2. **Object identity persists.** Delivery, shift, account and pass are stable entities; URLs, related records and history should reflect them. Current local tabs lose this context.
3. **Desktop board / phone worklist.** Linear's view choice transfers, but each forward move must still open PumpShield's guarded verification step. Do not post inventory on a drop.
4. **Evidence beside decision.** A receipt or photo belongs with its source measurement, review state and actor, not below multiple maps and slogans.
5. **Status is literal.** “Reported stock”, “last GPS report”, “draft pending verification” and “submitted” must not be upgraded to “live”, “approved” or “verified” by visual treatment.
6. **Density follows the task.** Owners compare queues/registers; Munshi enters numbers/photos under field conditions. They share tokens and semantics, not identical page compositions.

The recommended palette is an original proposal informed by these patterns, not copied from one of these products. No image generation was needed to establish these findings.
